## 😈 Cursed Features (The UX Crimes)

*   🖱️ **Lagging & Custom Cursor Traps**: The standard browser cursor is hidden and replaced with a custom glowing orb that floats, shakes, lags behind, and changes states upon hover.
*   🍼 **The Math Gauntlet (Add to Cart)**: 
    *   Clicking "Add to Cart" plays the famous *"Oh God, Ek Baar Dila De"* meme video under a cinematic letterbox mask for exactly 4 seconds.
    *   A high-level university math captcha then appears. 
    *   Failing the math gauntlet triggers a failed state playing dynamic sad memes (e.g., *Sad Ajay Devgan* or *Sad Homelander* for 4 seconds) before offering a mockingly easy "toddler-level" equation.
*   🛒 **Unfair Cart Logic**: Even if you solve the math gauntlet, the product added to your cart is actually the item *immediately below* the one you clicked on!
*   🔑 **Guest Mode Trap & Blood Covenant**:
    *   You can browse the store as a "Mortal Guest", but clicking **Logout** will trigger a system warning: `ERROR: You can never leave.` and spin the entire viewport 360 degrees using GSAP.
    *   To successfully unlock the ability to logout, you must enter the **Sign Up Page** and seal a customized, gold-trimmed **Blood Covenant** form.
*   ⚠️ **Glitchy "Destructive Search"**: A pulsating warning light in the header opens a secret search bar. Searching for any item is rigged: searching for one product automatically shifts results to display a completely different item.
*   🏃‍♂️ **Fleeing Footer Button**: The checkout button in the footer actively tracks your mouse cursor and flees in random directions whenever you try to hover or click it.
